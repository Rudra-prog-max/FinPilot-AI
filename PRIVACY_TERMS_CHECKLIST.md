# FinPilot-AI Privacy & Terms Launch Checklist

This document is a product-launch checklist, not legal advice.

## Privacy policy should explain

- What account data is collected (for example name and email).
- What financial data users enter or generate.
- Why the application processes that data.
- How authenticated data is isolated between users.
- Whether analytics, error monitoring, email, hosting, or AI providers receive data.
- Data retention and deletion practices.
- Security measures and their limitations.
- User rights and contact method.
- How policy changes are communicated.

## Terms should explain

- Acceptable use.
- Account responsibility.
- Service availability and limitations.
- Intellectual property.
- User-generated financial data responsibilities.
- Third-party service dependencies.
- Limitation of liability and governing terms appropriate to the deployment jurisdiction.

## Financial disclaimer

The product should clearly state that automated financial insights are informational and are not professional financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Users should independently verify important financial decisions with a qualified professional.

## Before launch

- [ ] Have the privacy policy reviewed for the actual jurisdiction and services used.
- [ ] Have terms reviewed for the actual jurisdiction and business structure.
- [ ] Add links to privacy and terms from registration and the public site.
- [ ] Provide an account/data deletion process appropriate to the product.
- [ ] Document retention and backup deletion behavior.
- [ ] Confirm every third-party provider used by production is reflected in the privacy disclosures.
